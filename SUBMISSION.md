## 1. Is AI-assisted work publishable?

Yes, under three conditions that almost every publisher now shares:

1. **AI cannot be an author.** Only humans can be authors (ICMJE/COPE
   consensus; SIAM, Springer Nature, Elsevier, Wiley all agree).
2. **Substantial AI use must be disclosed**, usually in the acknowledgements
   or methods. Our statement is at the end of the paper; `LOG.md` is the
   detailed record.
3. **The human author must be able to defend every part of the paper.**
   SIAM's policy (version 2.0, May 2026) says this explicitly. A referee or
   editor may ask any question about any proof or experiment.

What is *not* acceptable anywhere: hiding the AI's role, or claiming ideas
as one's own that one cannot explain.
